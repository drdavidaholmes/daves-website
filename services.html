How a commission works

  1. 01

    Brief and scope

    We begin with an initial consultation to discuss your anniversary, milestone, or project, alongside its target audience, timeline, and budget. You then receive a detailed written proposal outlining deliverables and fees.

  2. 02

    Research and verification

    Comprehensive archival and statistical research is conducted and cross-checked against primary sources, underpinned by full academic citations.

  3. 03

    Drafting and review

    Clear, engaging narrative or manuscript sections are delivered in agreed milestones, with structured review and feedback rounds built into the schedule.

  4. 04

    Final delivery

    You receive the publication-ready manuscript complete with full source notes and image documentation, formatted for immediate handover to your designer, publisher, or content team.
